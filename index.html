About me

Hi, I'm Haikong Lu, an undergraduate student in Applied Artificial Intelligence at The University of Hong Kong. I am currently an Undergraduate Research Assistant at HKU XLang Lab, supervised by Prof. Tao Yu.

My research interests lie in building reliable and generalizable AI systems. I am especially interested in Vision-Language-Action models, computer-use agents, and embodied AI, with an emphasis on realistic, long-horizon evaluation.

I enjoy working on agents that connect perception, language understanding, planning, and grounded action. Please feel free to reach out if you would like to discuss related research.
